IRON KESTREL '87 — AI-ASSISTED CONTENT DISCLOSURE

Iron Kestrel '87 contains pre-generated visual material created with assistance from OpenAI image-generation tools during development. This includes source artwork used for painted environments, aircraft, vehicles, ships, interface illustrations, effects and store key art. Selected output was cleaned, composited, resized, integrated and reviewed by the project before being bundled with the game.

Forty-eight foundational aircraft, weapon, impact, environment and warning sounds—including flight, VTOL, ejection, aircraft and naval weapons, servicing ambience, coastal weather, cockpit threat cues, radio transitions and interface feedback—are pre-generated ElevenLabs Sound Effects outputs selected and edited locally. Source candidates, prompts, checksums and processing records are retained by the project.

Eleven instrumental soundtrack cues are pre-generated Suno outputs created and downloaded under the project owner's paid Premier subscription, then edited, loudness-matched and looped locally. Source tracks, Suno generation identifiers, checksums and processing records are retained. The main title cue remains unproduced and is not represented by these files.

The game does not generate artwork, dialogue, audio or other content with generative AI while it is running. It does not send player prompts or gameplay data to an AI service.

Engineering and editorial work also used OpenAI Codex assistance. This development tooling is not a live player-facing AI system.

The maintained disclosure and provenance references are in docs/AI_GENERATION_DISCLOSURE.md, docs/asset-rights-register.json and art/source/generated-audio.
