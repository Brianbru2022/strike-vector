# Harrier '87 Art Bible

## Visual target

Painted 2D military illustration with readable silhouettes, restrained late-Cold-War colours and selective high-saturation highlights. The game should feel like 1987 box art brought to life, not a photorealistic simulator or a generic modern glass cockpit.

## Palette and lighting

- North Atlantic base: blue-grey, slate, sea green, muted ochre and fog white.
- NATO identification: cool grey forms with restrained white and desaturated blue.
- Warsaw Pact identification: olive, faded sand and oxide red accents.
- Interactive/HUD highlights: phosphor green; warnings: amber; immediate danger: signal red.
- Key light comes from the upper left unless a mission-specific time of day overrides it.

## Sprite rules

- Strict side profile for gameplay aircraft and vehicles.
- Preserve complete silhouettes and consistent world scale.
- Use painted shadow blocks and visible brush texture; avoid photographic noise.
- Provide normal, damaged and destroyed states before adding decorative variants.
- Transparent PNG source, cropped with 4–8% safety padding.
- Metadata defines source rectangle, centre pivot, display size and animation frames.

## Environment layers

1. Sky, celestial light and weather.
2. Distant islands, mountains and cloud banks.
3. Operational terrain, sea and target structures.
4. Foreground haze, spray, smoke and cockpit/HUD treatment.

## Interface

Use phosphor-green vector lines, radar sweeps, stencil labels and painted instrument surrounds. CRT scanlines are optional and never a substitute for authored art. Information must remain legible without colour alone and at a mobile landscape viewport of 844 × 390.
