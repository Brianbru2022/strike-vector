## North Cape assault panorama

- Runtime asset: `public/assets/backgrounds/arctic-north-cape-assault-operational-v1.webp`
- Archived source: `art/source/generated-1987/norwegian-mission-16-v1/north-cape-assault-panorama-source.png`
- Generation mode: built-in image generation

Exact prompt:

> Create an ultra-wide 3.2:1 painted 2D side-scrolling game environment panorama for a fictional 1987 North Cape assault. Side-on orthographic composition, cold polar dawn, restrained slate-blue, basalt-black and pale amber palette, visible traditional gouache and oil-brush texture, realistic but deliberately illustrated. The far left and far right must be open dark Norwegian Sea suitable for an aircraft carrier approach and recovery. Across the broad middle, form one continuous low coastal shelf rising into layered snow-streaked basalt cliffs; embed distant Soviet-era concrete command bunkers, radar masts, revetments and coastal batteries into the rock, with a dramatic hardened fortress near the centre-right. Add sea fog, low cloud, subtle surf, icy water highlights, smoke haze and deep atmospheric perspective. The lowest edge should provide an uninterrupted believable terrain/water contact silhouette for side-view ground sprites. No aircraft, no ships, no vehicles, no people, no explosions, no labels, no text, no HUD, no borders, no frames, no tiled/repeating texture, no obvious panorama seam. Full-bleed environment art.

The source PNG is retained unchanged. The runtime WebP is an RGB quality-84 export; gameplay contact remains governed by the authoritative `NORTH_CAPE_FORTRESS` height profile rather than painted geometry.
