# Iron Kestrel '87 Steam store-page draft

## Short description

Fly a Sea Harrier through a painted alternate-1987 North Atlantic war. Strike coastal defences, defend the fleet, operate from forward bases and master tense carrier recoveries in a tactical side-scrolling action campaign.

## About this game

**THE NORTH ATLANTIC, 1987. THE COLD WAR HAS TURNED HOT.**

Iron Kestrel '87 is a single-player tactical arcade flight game built around the unique character of the Sea Harrier. Launch from a light carrier, skim hostile coastlines, break radar and missile networks, protect forward bases and bring a damaged aircraft home.

### A complete combat deployment

- Fight through a sixteen-mission alternate-history campaign spanning carrier qualification, rescue, interdiction, fleet defence and the Bastion headquarters assault.
- Balance speed, altitude, fuel, weapons, countermeasures and aircraft damage without losing the clarity of accessible side-on controls.
- Recover aboard the carrier or at captured forward sites, then repair and rearm for the next sortie.

### Enemies that fight like aircraft

MiG-21s make fast crossing attacks. MiG-23s intercept from range. MiG-29 pairs coordinate and re-engage. Helicopters follow terrain while missile boats and shore batteries threaten the fleet. Detection, tracking and launch warnings give you time to read and answer the threat.

### Operations beyond the campaign

Complete the campaign to unlock deterministic three-sortie Dynamic Operations. Enter or share a seed, carry aircraft damage between sorties and pursue Gold, Silver or Bronze deployment records entirely offline.

### Painted 1987 presentation

Layered panoramas, illustrated aircraft and ships, period-inspired instruments, weather, smoke and weapon effects give the game a cohesive painted identity. Pre-generated AI-assisted visual source material was curated and edited for the shipped game; no generative AI runs during play.

Iron Kestrel '87 is an original fictional work. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with armed forces, aircraft manufacturers or earlier Harrier games.
