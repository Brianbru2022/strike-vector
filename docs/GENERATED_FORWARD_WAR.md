# Generated Forward War art

The mission 4–6 artwork was created with Codex's built-in image-generation mode. Runtime assets are deterministic and bundled; no image-generation endpoint is used during play.

## Runtime assets

- `public/assets/backgrounds/convoy-valley-painted-v1.webp` — mission 4 road valley, 1983 x 793.
- `public/assets/backgrounds/rescue-coast-painted-v1.webp` — mission 5 rescue coast, 1920 x 819.
- `public/assets/backgrounds/storm-fleet-painted-v1.webp` — mission 6 storm operating area, 1536 x 1024.
- `public/assets/sprites/explosion-stages-v2.png` — six-stage transparent explosion strip, 2100 x 362, with its original white matte removed for clean compositing.

Generated sources are retained under `art/source/generated-1987/forward-war-v1/`. The explosion source's pale preview checkerboard was removed with the existing edge-connected background-extraction process; its runtime corners have zero alpha.

## Prompt set

### Convoy valley

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen scrolling background for mission 4 of a painted 2D 1987 Harrier combat game. Primary request: a long North Atlantic island valley carrying a military road from left to right, broad enough for a moving armoured convoy, with stone walls, sparse crofts, a river crossing and distant basalt ridges. Style/medium: realistic hand-painted premium Cold War military game environment, natural brush texture and coherent scale. Composition/framing: wide side-on aircraft view, open air corridor, road and operational ground across the lower quarter, no abrupt foreground strip, strong distance layers. Lighting/mood: cold bright overcast morning, readable targets and restrained drama. Colour palette: slate blue, wet grey rock, muted moss and brown heather. Constraints: environment only; no vehicles, aircraft, people, UI, text, logos or watermark; no modern objects; suitable for wide horizontal crop and parallax.

### Rescue coast

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen scrolling background for mission 5 of a painted 2D 1987 Harrier combat game. Primary request: a remote North Atlantic rescue coast with a sheltered rocky beach, grass-covered headland, broken sea cliffs, a shallow inlet and distant misty islands. Style/medium: realistic hand-painted premium Cold War military aviation environment with natural coastal texture. Composition/framing: wide strict side view from low aircraft altitude, large open air corridor, a clear flat rescue area on land in the lower middle, sea-to-beach transition blended naturally. Lighting/mood: pale midday through sea mist, tense search-and-rescue atmosphere but highly readable. Colour palette: steel-blue sea, pale foam, muted olive grass, charcoal stone and cool mist. Constraints: environment only; no pilot, raft, vehicles, aircraft, buildings, UI, text, logos or watermark; suitable for horizontal crop and parallax.

### Storm fleet

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen scrolling background for mission 6 of a painted 2D 1987 Harrier combat game. Primary request: an exposed North Atlantic carrier operating area during a violent squall, immense dark sea with rolling whitecaps, layered storm clouds, rain shafts and a distant island silhouette. Constraints: environment only; no ships, aircraft, people, UI, text, logos or watermark; suitable for wide horizontal crop and parallax.

### Explosion stages

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent horizontal sprite sheet for explosions in a painted 2D 1987 combat game. Primary request: six sequential frames of one military fuel-and-metal explosion: ignition flash, expanding orange fireball, debris burst, rolling fire, dark smoke bloom, dissipating smoke. Style/medium: realistic hand-painted 2D game effects, cohesive Cold War key-art finish, crisp readable silhouette at small scale. Composition/framing: exactly six equal square cells in one perfectly straight horizontal row, explosion centred in every cell, identical scale and viewpoint, no gaps beyond cell padding. Lighting/mood: brilliant white-hot centre fading to orange flame, black-brown smoke and glowing embers. Background: genuine transparent alpha. Constraints: no checkerboard, white or coloured rectangle, ground, vehicle, aircraft, people, UI, text, borders, labels, logos or watermark; exactly six animation frames.
