# Iron Kestrel '87 title-clearance brief

## Status

**Pending formal clearance by a qualified trade-mark professional.** Repository and web searches are preliminary knockout checks only and must not be described as a legal clearance or opinion.

## Intended use and territories

- Mark: `IRON KESTREL '87` and punctuation/spacing variants.
- Goods/services: downloadable computer game software, entertainment software and related promotional material; likely Nice Classes 9 and 41, with Class 42 to be considered if online software services are later introduced.
- Proposed territories: United Kingdom, European Union, United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

## Required professional search scope

- Exact and confusingly similar word marks: `IRON KESTREL`, `IRON KESTREL 87`, `KESTREL 87`, `IRON + bird name`, phonetic variants and translated equivalents where relevant.
- Registered, pending, expired and common-law/unregistered uses covering games, software, entertainment and adjacent merchandising.
- Company names, domain names, major game storefronts, app stores and social handles.
- Device/logo search once the final logotype is fixed.

## Preliminary observations recorded 31 August 2026

- General web and major storefront searches did not identify an obvious released video game using the exact title.
- `Iron Kestrel` is already the name of an Australian tug vessel. That is a real prior commercial use, although it is not itself evidence of a conflicting computer-game mark.
- Searches of general web indexes cannot reliably prove absence from trade-mark registers or common-law use.

## Evidence required to close

Record the search provider, search date, territories/classes, marks searched, full opinion, limitations and approval decision in `config/commercial-approval.json`. Until then the commercial gate must remain blocked and no registration symbol should be used.
