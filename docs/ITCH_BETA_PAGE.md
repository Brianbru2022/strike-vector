# Iron Kestrel '87 — restricted itch.io beta page

## Page title

**IRON KESTREL '87 — Restricted Browser Beta**

## Short description

A painted Cold War VTOL action-simulation. Fly a Royal Navy Sea Harrier from HMS Invincible through carrier defence, low-level strike, rescue and Arctic operations across an alternative 1987 North Atlantic conflict.

## Beta description

This free restricted beta contains the current sixteen-mission campaign, carrier and forward-base recovery, aircraft damage and fuel management, three difficulty settings and unlockable seeded Dynamic Operations. It is being shared to learn whether the flight controls, guided opening, warnings, combat and recovery are enjoyable and understandable to real players.

This is a development build. Progress is stored in this browser profile. Export progress from **System Settings** before clearing browser data or changing devices.

## Controls

- A / D: turn and fly left or right.
- W / S: pitch up or down.
- V or Shift: toggle vectored-thrust hover mode.
- Space: twin 30 mm ADEN cannon.
- J or Z: fire the selected secondary weapon.
- Q or Tab: cycle secondary weapon.
- F: deploy countermeasure flares.
- Escape: pause.

Xbox-compatible controllers and touch controls are supported in the build but remain part of beta verification. Remapping and touch layout options are under **System Settings**.

## Recommended setup

Use a current Chromium, Firefox or Safari browser with hardware acceleration enabled. Landscape and fullscreen are recommended. Start with **Guided Campaign** if this is your first flight.

## Feedback request

Please report:

1. What control or objective first confused you?
2. Did you notice fighter, radar and missile warnings before taking damage?
3. If you failed, did you understand why?
4. Did carrier recovery feel tense, achievable or punitive?
5. After Mission 1, did you voluntarily want another sortie?
6. Which moment was most enjoyable?
7. Which moment made you consider stopping?

Attach **Export Beta Play Record** or **Export Issue Report** only if you are comfortable sharing it. Review the JSON first. The game never sends it automatically.

## Current known limitations

- Browser progress is local to the current site and browser profile.
- The itch iframe build deliberately does not install a service worker. This avoids cache and scope conflicts inside itch.io; the separate normal web build retains its installable offline PWA behaviour.
- Optional browser-generated radio speech varies by browser and operating system; captions are authoritative.
- This beta is not the signed Windows commercial edition and makes no Steam, achievement or cloud-save claim.

## AI-assisted content disclosure

Some shipped painted visual and audio source material was created with AI-assisted tools and then selected, edited, cleaned, integrated and audited by the developer. The game does not generate artwork or audio during play and does not require an AI service or account.
