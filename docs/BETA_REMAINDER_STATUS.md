# Beta-first remainder status

This document separates locally implemented preparation from work that requires genuine people, hosted services or professional approval.

## Locally implemented or already present

- Guided Mission 1 uses action-driven steps, explicit success transitions, skip support and device-aware prompts.
- Directional controls share semantic actions across keyboard, gamepad and touch.
- The HUD selects one centre notification by priority; detailed recovery, weapon and threat information is contextual.
- Aircraft and targets use presentation-only silhouette scaling, rim separation, depth treatment and authoritative terrain-contact shadows without changing collision geometry.
- Combat feedback identifies missile direction, fighter state, hit outcome, weapon and damage direction.
- Whole-sortie retry remains authoritative, with phase-specific restart guidance for staged missions.
- Campaign and operation diagnostics record simulation time, result, weapons, damage, warnings and landing grades locally.
- The restricted itch target now records only coarse beta behaviour locally and supports deliberate JSON export.
- The dedicated itch build uses relative assets, excludes internal tools and service workers, is tested under a nested iframe path and packages `index.html` at the ZIP root.
- Legal, asset, Windows, rollback, Steam and commercial-release work packages remain separate from the external-beta gate.

## Cannot be completed by automation

- Five genuine internal play sessions, including three novices and two experienced players.
- Judgement about tutorial wording, HUD obstruction, pacing and late-failure frustration based on those sessions.
- Physical keyboard, Xbox-compatible controller and real touch-device certification.
- Listening approval on headphones, ordinary speakers and a laptop/mobile device.
- A live feedback destination chosen and controlled by the developer.
- Uploading and smoke-testing the restricted itch page in the developer's account.
- Behavioural evidence from strangers and decisions based on it.
- Formal title/trademark and legal approval, code signing, Windows 10/11 lifecycle testing, Steamworks review, tax/banking setup, final soak and release freeze.

These items must stay pending. Their templates and gates exist so a missing human or external result cannot be mistaken for completion.
