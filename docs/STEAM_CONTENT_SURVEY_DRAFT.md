# Steam content survey working draft

Prepared 31 August 2026 from the candidate repository. This is an answer brief for the Steamworks account holder, not evidence of submission or Valve approval.

## General content

- Single-player military arcade action set in a fictional alternate 1987 conflict.
- Frequent non-graphic combat involving aircraft, vehicles, ships, missiles, bombs and gunfire.
- Explosions, damaged vehicles, ejection and persistent wreckage are depicted without blood, gore or civilian injury detail.
- No gambling, sexual content, nudity, drug use, user-generated content, chat or in-game purchases are implemented.
- The final answers must be checked against every accessible build branch, because uploaded but inaccessible mature content must also be disclosed.

## Generative artificial intelligence

Select **Pre-Generated AI Content: Yes**.

Suggested accurate description:

> Pre-generated visual source material was created with assistance from OpenAI image-generation tools during development. It is used in painted environments, aircraft, vehicles, ships, interface illustrations, visual effects and store key art. Outputs were curated and locally edited for composition, transparency, scale, contact alignment, damage states and colour consistency before being bundled with the game. The game does not generate content with AI at runtime and does not connect players to a generative-AI service.

Select **Live-Generated AI Content: No**.

The Steamworks account holder must compare this description with the exact build and store media submitted for review. Any later addition of AI-assisted localisation, narrative, audio or imagery requires the survey and disclosure record to be reassessed.

## Store/build consistency checks

- Store screenshots must be captured gameplay, not the generated key art.
- Capsules may use the pre-generated, project-edited store key art and must therefore remain within the disclosed scope.
- Do not select achievements, Steam Cloud, full controller support, Steam Deck compatibility, additional languages or accessibility conformance until their corresponding certification record is approved.
