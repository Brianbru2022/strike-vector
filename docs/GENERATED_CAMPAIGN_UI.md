# Campaign UI artwork

The campaign chart was generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation mode and then integrated as a deterministic bundled game asset.

## Final prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-screen campaign operations map background for a modern-retro 1987 side-scrolling Harrier game
Primary request: a painted top-down North Atlantic naval operations chart showing a fictional five-sector island chain running from a NATO carrier group in the lower-left across stormy water to a fortified Warsaw Pact command island in the upper-right
Scene/backdrop: dark North Atlantic ocean with subtle bathymetric contours, rugged volcanic islands, fjords, beaches, airfields and harbour silhouettes; faint paper chart grid, compass rose, latitude lines and restrained analogue plotting marks
Style/medium: hand-painted 2D strategy-game map fused with a 1980s naval command-room chart; premium modern lighting and texture, legible silhouettes, not photorealistic
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape; five clearly separated island sectors arranged on a gentle rising diagonal route; ample dark water and negative space around the route for HTML labels and mission cards; carrier silhouette at far left and ominous command complex silhouette at far right
Lighting/mood: cold green-blue night chart illumination with soft phosphor glow, small amber dawn highlights on coastlines, tense Cold War atmosphere
Color palette: near-black navy, slate blue, desaturated moss, phosphor green and restrained amber
Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI panels, no buttons, no borders, no watermark, no modern satellite imagery, no national flags, no repeated island shapes
```

## Mission briefing artwork

The briefing hero was generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation mode. The runtime JPEG is quality-optimised while the original PNG is retained in the source archive.

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: cinematic mission-intelligence artwork for a 1987 Cold War Harrier game briefing screen
Primary request: a Royal Navy Sea Harrier FRS.1 launching at dawn from the ski-jump of an Invincible-class aircraft carrier, flying towards a rugged fictional North Atlantic island where a Soviet-era coastal radar dish, mobile SA-6 missile battery and fortified fuel depot are visible as distant tactical targets
Scene/backdrop: storm-dark North Atlantic water, steep volcanic cliffs, low cloud and cold dawn light breaking along the horizon; deck crew and carrier details kept small and secondary
Style/medium: premium hand-painted 2D game concept art with controlled brush texture, strong silhouettes and believable 1980s military detail; modern-retro presentation, not photorealistic
Composition/framing: wide cinematic landscape; Sea Harrier prominent in the left-centre lifting off towards the island on the right; clear visual travel direction left to right; layered depth; leave darker negative space near the edges for UI overlays
Lighting/mood: tense pre-combat dawn, cold blue-green shadows, restrained amber sunlight, subtle exhaust haze and sea spray
Colour palette: charcoal navy, slate blue, muted olive, phosphor green accents and restrained amber
Constraints: historically plausible 1987 equipment; no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI panels, no border, no watermark, no modern stealth aircraft, no missiles being fired, no explosions
```
