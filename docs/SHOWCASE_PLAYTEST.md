# Showcase playtest evidence

Automated checks establish deterministic behaviour and technical validity. Only genuine first-time sessions count towards the human targets below.

## Required sessions

- Eight first-time players in total.
- At least three sessions at 844 × 390 mobile landscape using touch controls.
- Every player attempts guided Mission 1; the group also covers Missions 6 and 10 on Arcade, Pilot and Veteran.
- Export Local Playtest Diagnostics after each session and record the build identifier without collecting names or raw inputs.

## Target outcomes

- At least 80% complete guided Mission 1 without outside instruction.
- At least 75% identify a fighter or missile warning before impact.
- No unavoidable opening attack, impossible recovery or objective blocker.
- No repeated report of unclear controls, unexplained failure or unreadable targets.

## Ranked validation risks

1. Mission 6 may overload a new player with fighters, sea-skimmers and carrier integrity warnings simultaneously.
2. Mission 10 may not communicate the locked-target sequence clearly during its first three phases.
3. Carrier recovery may remain too demanding after combat damage on Pilot or Veteran.
4. Compact touch controls may obscure threats near the lower corners.
5. Strong storm grading may reduce hostile-aircraft silhouette contrast.

These are test hypotheses, not recorded defects. Do not mark a target as passed without an actual player session.
